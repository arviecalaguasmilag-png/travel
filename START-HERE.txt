TRAVEL

App: https://taiwan-together-production.up.railway.app
Source: https://github.com/arviecalaguasmilag-png/travel

The app is called Travel. The existing address remains the same to preserve
your saved browser data and bookmarks.

YOUR NEXT COUNTRY

Choose New trip. Enter a trip name, country or region, and the time zone for
your destination city. Add places under Saved places. Use full addresses with
the city and country. Switch trips using Your trip; each keeps its own places.
Edit next to the trip dropdown changes its name, country, and time zone.
Saved places includes Edit and Remove controls for individual places.
Your original Taiwan places are preserved in the first Taiwan trip.

DIRECTIONS

Choose From and To, then Bus / train, Driving, or Walking. The green button
opens Google Maps. Open a suggested route there for its departures, service
numbers, direction, boarding stop, and getting-off stop, where available.
To compare Uber, use Copy destination, then Open Uber. Paste the destination
and confirm pickup, the exact destination, fare, and waiting time before booking.
Travel does not automatically decide the best transport option for you.

SHARING AND BACKUPS

Saved places > Share this trip copies that trip's settings and places to a
link. Send it to your travel buddy. They review it and choose Add these trips.
Edits stay on each phone until you share again. Anyone with a shared link can
read its trip settings, names, and addresses.
Back up all trips downloads a JSON file. Import backup restores trips as
separate copies, keeping the trips you already have. Older Taiwan backups
and links also work. Keep a backup before clearing browser data.

ON YOUR PHONE

Open the app in Safari or Chrome and use Add to Home Screen. Load it once
online. Saved addresses and app files can work offline afterward; directions
and ride booking need internet. The app runs without this chat or computer.

COST AND HOSTING

No Google Cloud API key, Google prepayment, database, or Make subscription is
needed. Railway's free hosting allowance is finite and service can pause when
credits are exhausted. Sleep when inactive is enabled to reduce usage.
No paid account upgrade was requested or made for this app.
If the site is unavailable, try reloading after a brief cold start, or open
Google Maps/Uber directly and use your saved addresses or backup.

FUTURE DEPLOYMENTS

The GitHub repository is arviecalaguasmilag-png/travel, branch main.
Railway's GitHub connection deploys updates to that branch. Dockerfile defines
the build. Runtime settings are saved in Railway: Serverless enabled,
/health healthcheck with a 90-second timeout, and 3 On Failure restart retries.
No API secrets are needed.
Node 24 can preview the source with node server.mjs; tests run with node --test.
