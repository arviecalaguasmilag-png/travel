      Travel help

      • A trip for any country. Use New trip to choose a country and time zone. Switch trips using the dropdown. Edit changes the current trip’s settings; its places stay saved.
      • Add your places. Enter full addresses including the city and country. Saved places has Edit and Remove controls. Share this trip copies its places and settings to a travel buddy; later changes stay on the phone where you make them.
      • Directions open in Google Maps. Choose bus/train, driving, or walking, then tap the green button. Maps shows routes and departure information where available. This app does not choose the fastest or cheapest option.
      • Using My location. Google Maps asks to use your location. This app does not track you.
      • Checking Uber. Copy the destination, open Uber, and paste it. Verify pickup, destination, fare, and availability before booking.
      • Keep your trips. Add Travel to your home screen. Back up all trips before clearing browser data. The app and saved addresses can work offline after an online visit; directions and booking need internet.
