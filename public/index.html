      A little help for your trip

      • A trip for any country. Choose New trip, set its country and time zone, then add your places. Switch trips at the top.
      • Find your way. Search by place name, local name, or address. Choose bus/train, driving, or walking and open Google Maps for routes and departure information where available. My location lets Maps find you; Travel does not track you.
      • Keep places handy. Add full addresses with the city and country. Select a saved place to use it as a destination. Undo brings back a place you just removed.
      • Think in pesos. The converter suggests your trip’s local currency. Change it if needed. Enter a price to see an estimate in PHP. The rate date is shown; offline results use the last saved rate. Bank and exchange fees are not included.
      • Share and back up. Open the ••• Trip tools menu. Shared links copy a trip; later edits stay on the device where you make them. Importing adds separate copies. Back up before clearing browser data.
      • Take Travel with you. Add it to your home screen. After an online visit, saved places and previously loaded rates work offline. Google Maps needs internet.
