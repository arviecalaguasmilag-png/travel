Privacy & data

Travel saves places for your trips and opens directions in other services. It has no account, paid map API, advertising, or analytics.

  • Your trips, countries, time zones, and places stay in this browser’s local storage. They are not uploaded to the app server. Clearing browser data or using private browsing may remove them.
  • Your previous Taiwan Together places are copied into your first Taiwan trip. The original local data is retained during migration.
  • Share this trip creates a link with its settings and places after the # character. This fragment is not sent to our web server. Anyone with the link can read and import it.
  • Back up all trips downloads a JSON file containing every trip. Importing a backup or shared link adds separate trips after you confirm; it does not overwrite your existing trips.
  • Opening Google Maps sends the selected addresses to Google. My location leaves the origin for Maps to determine; this app does not request or track your position.
  • Opening Uber connects to Uber. You enter or paste your destination there. These services have their own privacy policies and may require accounts.
  • A service worker stores app files for offline use. No Google Maps content is cached by this app. Hosting providers may keep ordinary access logs such as IP addresses.

Travel information

This app does not calculate route comparisons, fares, or departures. Google Maps provides directions where available; Uber provides its own booking details. Availability varies by country. Check current signs and operator information.

External services are subject to Google Maps terms, Google’s privacy policy, and Uber’s privacy notice. Travel is not affiliated with Google, Uber, or transit operators.
