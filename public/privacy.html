Privacy & data

Travel saves places for your trips, opens directions in Google Maps, and estimates local prices in Philippine pesos. It has no account, advertising, or analytics.

  • Your trips, countries, time zones, currency preferences, and places stay in this browser’s local storage. They are not uploaded to the app server. Clearing browser data or using private browsing may remove them.
  • Your previous Taiwan Together places are copied into your first Taiwan trip. The original local data is retained during migration.
  • Share this trip creates a link with its settings and places after the # character. This fragment is not sent to our web server. Anyone with the link can read and import it. Later edits stay on the device where you make them.
  • Back up all trips downloads a JSON file containing every trip. Importing a backup or shared link adds separate trips after you confirm; it does not overwrite existing trips.
  • Opening Google Maps sends the selected addresses to Google. My location leaves the origin for Maps to determine; Travel does not request or track your position.
  • The currency converter sends only the selected currency code to our server, which requests a reference rate from Frankfurter. Entered amounts and trip addresses are not sent. Validated rates are cached on the server and in your browser for reuse. The original rate date and offline or unavailable-update status are displayed.
  • A service worker stores app files for offline use. No Google Maps content is cached by Travel. Hosting providers may keep ordinary access logs such as IP addresses.

Travel information

Travel does not calculate route comparisons, fares, or departures. Google Maps provides directions where available. Availability varies by country. Check current signs and operator information.

Currency estimates

Conversions use daily reference rates from Frankfurter. The date shown is the rate’s date, not a live market quote. Offline conversions use a previously saved rate, which may be outdated. Actual bank, card, and money-changer rates and fees may differ.

Google Maps is subject to Google Maps terms and Google’s privacy policy. Travel is not affiliated with Google, Frankfurter, or transit operators.
